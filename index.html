How to Play

The board is scratched onto the mud with limestone — 22 points joined by lines, with four little triangles at the corners.

  • 4 tigers begin fixed on their points (top row, or the centre spine). 16 goats wait beside the board.
  • Goats go first. On each turn a goat is placed on any empty point. Goats cannot move until all 16 are placed (2 points stay empty).
  • A tiger slides to a joined empty point, or leaps straight over one goat into an empty point beyond — that goat is eaten.
  • Tigers cannot jump other tigers, and goats never jump.
  • Tigers win by eating 6 goats. Goats win by trapping every tiger so none can move.

Controls: tap a point to place a goat; tap your piece then a highlighted point to move. Pinch/scroll is off — the whole board always stays in view.
